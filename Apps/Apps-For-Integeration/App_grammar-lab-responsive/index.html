A1Foundations & other•Verb be: am/is/are

Can-do goal

I can identify and describe people, places, feelings and situations with be.

Controlled recall

Correct the sentence: I am agree.

1

Rule and pattern

⌄

Be links the subject to an identity, description, location, age or condition.

✓Affirmative
I am / he, she, it is / you, we, they are

Use: give an identity or description

Example: My study room is quiet.

−Negative
subject + am/is/are + not

Use: say a description is not true

Example: The exercises are not difficult today.

?Yes/no question
Am/Is/Are + subject + complement?

Use: check an identity, condition or place

Example: Is the next lesson ready?

??Wh-question
Wh-word + am/is/are + subject?

Use: ask for specific information

Example: Where are your notes?

◔Short answer
Yes, subject + am/is/are. / No, subject + am/is/are not.

Use: answer naturally and briefly

Example: No, they are not.

2♧Examples and contrast ⌄
Compare: “I agree” describes an action or opinion. “I am happy” uses be to link the subject to a description.
3⇄Learning path ⌄
Recall the rule, correct an example, say it aloud, and then use it in a personal sentence.
4△Typical mistake ⌄
Do not add am/is/are before verbs such as agree: say “I agree,” not “I am agree.”
5▤Resources and extra practice ⌄
Create five affirmative sentences and turn each one into a negative and a question.
6✧Optional AI explanation ⌄
Ask for another explanation only after attempting the recall task yourself.

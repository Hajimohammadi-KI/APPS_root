# Grammar material sources

The complete explanations in the Grammar Lab were newly written after reviewing
the user-provided [Google Drive course-material folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1isE3OWBFZcr9eRDwWvNXHWb5vo0qmGAF?usp=drive_link).
The application does not redistribute book pages or copy their exercises. It
indexes the distinct source families and links back to the originals, while
hiding duplicate compressed or merged copies from the learner-facing catalog.

## External curriculum cross-check

The level progression was also checked against current, publicly available
curriculum and assessment references:

- [Deutsche Welle: Nicos Weg A1 grammar overview](https://static.dw.com/downloads/52718691/grammar-overview-nicos-weg-a1.pdf)
- [Deutsche Welle: Nicos Weg A2 grammar overview](https://static.dw.com/downloads/59838099/grammatikuebersicht-nicos-weg-a2.pdf)
- [Deutsche Welle: Nicos Weg B1 grammar overview](https://static.dw.com/downloads/59838242/grammatikuebersicht-nicos-weg-b1.pdf)
- [telc: Grammatiktraining Deutsch B2](https://shop.telc.net/media/catalog/product/file//v/0/v01-4004-tgr-1801a_grammatiktraining_b2.pdf)
- [Goethe-Institut: CEFR levels A1–C2](https://www.goethe.de/de/spr/kur/stu.html)
- [Goethe-Institut: Goethe-Zertifikat C2 objectives](https://www.goethe.de/resources/files/pdf80/C2_Handbuch_Pruefziele131112111.pdf)

The CEFR describes communicative competence rather than prescribing one
exclusive grammar inventory. For that reason, the app preserves the proven
14-unit progression at every level and uses multi-label categories. A topic
such as “Verben mit Präpositionen” is correctly discoverable under both verbs
and prepositions. The category selector only offers categories that contain
topics at the selected level, so changing levels cannot leave a stale,
artificially empty result.

The English Present Perfect comparison page is used only to identify possible
false transfer. German explanations and exercises follow the German contrast:
completed events and present results may use Perfekt, while a state that still
continues with `seit` uses Präsens.

## Explanation contract

Every one of the 84 grammar units contains:

1. a purpose-oriented overview;
2. formation and paradigm guidance;
3. usage and meaning distinctions;
4. sentence-position guidance;
5. exceptions and contrasts;
6. a memory aid;
7. examples and a typical-error correction;
8. at least five controlled exercises with correction, completion and
   sentence-order retrieval;
9. three independent writing prompts, each followed by spoken confirmation;
10. links to the supplied material and direct online
    explanation/exercise pages.

Automated content tests require every section to be populated and require every
CEFR level to have an attributed source. The source books remain reference
material; all learner-facing wording in the application is original.

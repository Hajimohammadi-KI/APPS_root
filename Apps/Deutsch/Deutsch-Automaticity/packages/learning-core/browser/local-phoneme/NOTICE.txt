Only waveform-to-phoneme inference is used. No phoneme-to-word model or target-text correction is loaded.
Training-data overlap, accuracy on Persian speakers and pronunciation diagnosis have not been established. Outputs are unverified acoustic estimates, never a score or mastery decision.

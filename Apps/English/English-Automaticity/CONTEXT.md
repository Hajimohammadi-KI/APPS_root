# English Grammar Automaticity Context

## Self-Declared Level

The A1–C2 level selected by the learner as the starting point. This choice
filters the learning path but does not certify proficiency.

## Placement Check

An optional diagnostic that recommends a Self-Declared Level. Skipping it does
not block learning.

## Verified Level

The highest CEFR level for which the Graduation Gate has been satisfied.

## Graduation Gate

The proof required to verify a level: repeated successful writing and speaking,
spontaneous production, delayed retrieval, strong grammar accuracy, and no
unresolved critical error.

## Automaticity

The ability to use a target grammar pattern accurately under time pressure and
in transfer tasks, not only in recognition exercises.

## Repair Mode

Focused practice generated when the learner's evidence is weaker than the
chosen level. It supports the learner without silently changing their level.

# English Grammar Automaticity

## Short description

Build automatic English grammar through production, correction, repair,
transfer, and spaced review.

## Full description

English Grammar Automaticity is a complete A1–C2 learning system for grammar
and speaking. It turns grammar knowledge into usable, automatic language with
guided retrieval, controlled practice, original production, correction,
spoken repair, transfer tasks, and scheduled review.

The app includes:

- 112 grammar units, each with at least five exercises.
- 72 guided conversation topics across A1–C2.
- A seven-stage daily automaticity path.
- A 15-unit B1–C1 thesis speaking and writing sprint.
- Exact-topic explanations and practice resources.
- Speech, local audio recordings, error repair, progress, and import/export.
- Offline-first practice after the initial load.

Most learning data remains on the device. Full grammar assessment requires an
internet connection.

# Visual assets

Both illustrations were created with Codex's built-in image-generation mode and
are used by the redesigned dashboard.

## Automaticity journey hero

- Saved file: `apps/web/public/automaticity-journey-hero.png`
- Use case: stylized-concept
- Prompt:

> Asset type: premium learning-app home hero illustration. Create a beautiful,
> optimistic editorial 3D illustration for an English grammar automaticity
> learning app. Show one adult learner progressing along a luminous curved
> pathway from a notebook and grammar cards, through a speaking microphone and
> friendly conversation bubbles, toward a confident presentation moment with a
> subtle achievement glow. Use an airy deep-blue-to-indigo environment with
> layered translucent shapes, small teal and warm-coral accents, and generous
> quiet negative space on the left for website copy. Include an inclusive adult
> learner, laptop, notebook, microphone, abstract speech bubbles, and small
> progress markers. Use premium soft 3D editorial illustration, elegant rounded
> forms, tactile paper-and-clay materials, and a sophisticated SaaS product
> aesthetic. Compose a wide landscape with primary visual weight on the right
> two-thirds and a clear flowing left-to-right progression. Use luminous,
> welcoming, ambitious, calm lighting with soft cinematic depth. Palette: navy
> #102a56, royal blue #1760df, sky blue, teal #18a786, warm coral #ff8a65, and
> cream highlights. No logos, UI screenshots, readable text, letters,
> watermarks, flags, photorealism, clutter, or cropped faces and objects.

## Progress story

- Saved file: `apps/web/public/automaticity-progress-story.png`
- Use case: stylized-concept
- Prompt:

> Asset type: three-part visual progress strip for a premium English learning
> dashboard. Create one cohesive horizontal illustration containing three
> clearly separated but flowing progress scenes: active recall with grammar
> cards and a glowing notebook; confident speaking with a microphone and
> conversation bubbles; and mastery with an achievement path, subtle star, and
> confident presentation silhouette. Use a light airy cream-to-pale-blue
> background with three rounded editorial vignette zones connected by one thin
> luminous blue-to-teal path. Show learning objects and one consistent adult
> learner across the three stages; no children. Use premium soft 3D editorial
> illustration, elegant clay-and-paper materials, and a crisp, sophisticated
> educational SaaS aesthetic. Compose a wide landscape triptych with equal
> visual weight, generous padding, and crops suitable for three dashboard cards.
> Lighting should feel bright, focused, encouraging, and calmly successful.
> Palette: royal blue #1760df, sky blue, teal #18a786, warm coral #ff8a65,
> cream, and navy accents. No readable text, letters, numbers, logos,
> watermarks, UI screenshots, clutter, or dark background.
